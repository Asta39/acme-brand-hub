The logo.

Two leaves meet to form an A. Simple enough to read on a box from across a cold room.

Space

Keep one leaf's width clear on every side.

Size

At least 24 mm in print, 120 px on screen.

Colour

Green on white, or white on green. Nothing else.

Don't

Stretch it, add effects, or place it on busy flowers.

Mostly white.
A little green.

White lets the flowers carry the colour. Green is the brand. Butter marks the label.
